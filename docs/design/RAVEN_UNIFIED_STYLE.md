# Raven interface direction

- Audience: a small, private research group reading source material on desktop, with mobile review
- First screen: collect material on your device, inspect sources, then choose what enters the graph
- Reference: a dark archival desk and an engraved instrument panel, rather than a generic SaaS dashboard
- Recognition: heavier self-hosted Manrope, near-black surfaces with narrow silver rims, generous headings with compact evidence cards
- No invented source counts, company, social links, testimonials or claims of verified identity

The owner requests no final full stops in display copy and at paragraph endings. Internal sentences,
addresses, file extensions and user-authored evidence retain their punctuation. Legal pages describe
actual data handling for local use and private server deployment; they do not invent an operator or
promise a universal legal basis. Existing brand colors override generic skill palette suggestions.

Motion serves a state change or the requested rotating Raven sculpture. The sculpture has a pause
control, suspends offscreen and in hidden tabs, and falls back to a static image for reduced motion
or unavailable WebGL. Use one bounded GPU buffer set, cap resolution and draw at no more than 30 fps.
Canvas positions continue to come from the existing layout engine and accepted proposals.

The supplied `human-web-design` SKILL.md was applied. Its referenced scripts and reference files
were not attached or found in the available bundles/code search, so no claim is made to running
those missing scripts. `scripts/design/audit-display-copy.mjs` is Raven's own TypeScript AST audit
of authored JSX text and display attributes, supplemented by manual string review and browser QA.
It excludes tests and does not rewrite user records, URLs, quotations or licence texts.

Baseline examples: `WelcomePage.tsx:43` ended the product name with a period; `ExecutionView.tsx:25`
used emoji for state; `nodeStore.ts:44` re-read the entire node list on every content update.
Brand silver and the investigator's dense canvas are deliberate choices, not generic palette findings.
